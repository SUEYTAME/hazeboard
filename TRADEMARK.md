# Hazeboard Trademark Policy

This document explains how the "Hazeboard" name and brand may be used.
It complements — not replaces — the MIT [LICENSE](LICENSE) that governs
the source code.

## TL;DR

- **MIT covers the code.** Use it, modify it, fork it, build on it.
- **This policy covers the name and brand.** It is permissive for
  community use and reserved for product naming and endorsement.
- **In doubt? Ask** by opening an issue titled `Trademark request` in this
  repository.

## The relationship with the MIT license

The MIT license grants broad freedoms to use, copy, modify, and
redistribute the source code. It does not grant rights to the name
"Hazeboard," its visual identity, or any associated brand assets. Those
are reserved by the project's author, Edgar Homero Sanchez Gonzalez.

This is the model used by most mature open-source projects: the code is
free, the brand is stewarded.

## What you can do without asking

- **Forking and modifying the code** — per MIT, no permission needed.
- **Describing compatibility and origin** — "based on Hazeboard," "fork
  of Hazeboard," "works with Hazeboard" are welcome, with attribution.
- **Naming a fork distinctly** — pick your own product name; mention
  Hazeboard as its origin if you like.
- **Educational, journalistic, and personal use** — posts, tutorials,
  videos, talks, papers.
- **Community contributions** — using the name freely in issues and pull
  requests.

## What requires written permission

These uses cause, or can reasonably be perceived to cause, confusion
about official affiliation, endorsement, or sponsorship:

- **Product, service, or company names** that include "Hazeboard" or a
  close variant ("Hazeboard Pro," "Hazeboard Cloud," "HazeBoard for X").
- **Endorsement or affiliation claims** — "Official Hazeboard,"
  "Powered by Hazeboard," "Partner of Hazeboard."
- **Domain names, social handles, and app store listings** containing
  "Hazeboard" or a close variant.
- **Logos, wordmarks, and visual identity assets** in any commercial
  context.

The principle: if your use suggests an official relationship that
doesn't exist, please ask.

## How to request permission

Open an issue in this repository titled **"Trademark request — [your use
case]"** describing what you want to use the name for, where it would
appear, and for how long. Permission, when granted, is non-exclusive,
revocable, and limited to the use described.

## Modeled after

[PostgreSQL Trademark Policy](https://www.postgresql.org/about/policies/trademarks/),
[Apache Software Foundation Trademark Policy](https://www.apache.org/foundation/marks/),
and [Mozilla Trademark Policy](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/foundation/trademarks/policy/).
